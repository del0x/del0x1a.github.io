I got into Whiskey a while back through a long-time friend of mine who used to be in the business of selling Whiskeys. So as a newcomer to the hobby,
I ended up jumping straight into it without researching and getting a better understanding of the underlying process of making Whiskey. Well, to be fair I did visit distilleries where they go over the process, it just never really stuck since I wasn't interested in making Whiskey by myself but rather drinking and enjoying them! 

I aim to dig a bit deeper into the process and also look into some of my favorite distilleries so far, in my attempt to actually educate myself.
